Calculation notes — verify before publishing

Longevity is provisionally calculated at $0.25 per company year per hour (maximum 25 years). Confirm the exact service-credit rule against the TA.

OT/DT uses salary plus 11.75% license percentage and provisionally includes longevity. The $370 license flat amount and qualification overrides are excluded. Holiday and ECB use that same provisional hourly rate.

Shift differential: $2.12/hr for 1100–1659 and $4.42/hr for 1700–0459. Liaison remains $350/month. Move pay uses the 1.5%, 11.75%, and 8.25% rules.

Holiday Off and Holiday Worked count toward biweekly gross, but are excluded from the annualized calculation.
